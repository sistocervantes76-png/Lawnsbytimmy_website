Standard Mowing Scope & Quality Matrix

Every visit follows the same four-point checklist, so your lawn looks the same crisp way every single time.

1

Precision Turf Mowing

Set to optimal turf health heights (3.5" to 4.0") to protect against summer heat.

2

Hardscape Edging

Crisp vertical lines along driveways, sidewalks, and patios.

3

Line Trimming

Detailed string trimming around fences, trees, garden beds, and obstacles.

4

Hard Surface Blow-off

Driveways, walkways, and patios thoroughly cleared of grass clippings before leaving.

Note: Lawns untrimmed for over 10 days require a one-time catch-up service to restore baseline turf health before recurring plan rates apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you handle gate codes and pets?

Access codes are stored securely in our field app. Pets must be secured indoors and gates unlocked on scheduled route days.

What is your rain policy?

If heavy rain delays service, routes shift by 24 to 48 hours as soon as the soil dries to prevent ground ruts.

How does billing work?

We collect a card on file during account setup and process payment automatically after each completed service visit.
