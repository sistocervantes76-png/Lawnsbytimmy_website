Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Lawns by Timmy ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website lawnsbytimmy.com and provides residential lawn mowing and property maintenance services in Oakland Park and Fort Lauderdale, Florida. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or become a customer, we collect information you give us directly:

  • Name, mobile phone number, and email address
  • Service property address and details about the property (lot size, gate access, pets)
  • Services requested and any add-ons selected
  • Your consent preferences for text messaging
  • Payment card information, which is collected and stored by our payment processor — we never store full card numbers ourselves

How we use your information

  • To prepare and send you a quote
  • To schedule, perform, and confirm lawn service visits
  • To send appointment reminders, service alerts, and billing confirmations
  • To process payments for services rendered
  • To respond when you contact us

SMS text messaging & consent

If you check the SMS consent box on our web forms or quote request forms, you agree to receive automated text messages from Lawns by Timmy regarding your service — including appointment reminders, service alerts, and billing confirmations.

  • Unchecked Box Requirement: Opt-in to receive text messages is entirely voluntary. The consent checkbox on our forms is unchecked by default and requires an explicit action on your part to opt in.
  • Non-Condition of Purchase: Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.
  • Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your service schedule.
  • Rates: Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier.
  • Opt-Out (STOP): You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP to any text message. You will receive one final message confirming your opt-out.
  • Support (HELP): Reply HELP for assistance, or call us directly at (954) 880-3500.
  • Carrier Liability: Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Strict Mobile Data Non-Sharing Policy: We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your mobile phone number, or your consent to receive text messages, with any third party or affiliate for marketing or commercial purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are strictly excluded from any third-party data sharing practices.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the business, and only as needed for them to perform that work:

  • Our telephone and text messaging provider, to deliver calls and messages
  • Our payment processor, to bill for services
  • Our scheduling and customer record systems

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our legal rights.

Data retention

We keep customer records for as long as you remain a customer, and afterward as needed for tax, accounting, and legal purposes. You may request deletion of your records at any time using the contact details below.

Your choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP to any message, or contact us directly.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link, or contact us.
  • Access or deletion: email or call us and we will honor reasonable requests.

Children's privacy

Our services are directed to homeowners and property owners. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
