## In plain words
Introductory discounts and “starting at” mowing prices are easier to find than clearly priced multi-visit packages. Quality Lawncare advertises 50% off the first cut in Pompano Beach; Evergreen advertises $50 off a new pest or lawn agreement in Oakland Park. LawnGuru has a particularly clear recurring-service promotion: $15 off as three $5 credits, not $15 off one cut. LawnStarter leads its Fort Lauderdale page with “starting at $19,” but that is not identified there as a $19 first-cut promotion. Lawn Bros publishes four package amounts, but does not explain their billing period, and its homepage conflicts with the package page on visit frequency. Treatment plans from TruGreen are not mowing packages and must be kept separate in comparisons.

No dated 2026 Black Friday or seasonal lawn-service discount was verified in this scan. One local land-clearing page has Black Friday in its title, but merely tells readers to ask contractors about holiday deals; it does not publish an actual offer. This is a bounded search finding, not proof that no company has a promotion elsewhere. “Advertised now” means visible on a page accessed today, not independently confirmed availability, eligibility, or freshness.
